Account deletion

The standard HoleFilled closed beta does not enable account creation or collect personal information in the app. There is therefore no beta account or beta profile to delete.

If you received an authenticated test build

Do not submit account details in a public issue. Request deletion through the private contact method supplied by the beta organizer who gave you access. The organizer must verify the request, delete the corresponding authentication account and beta data, and confirm completion.

Before production launch

This page will be replaced with a self-service or private deletion request process, including the developer identity, contact method, deletion timeline, and any legally required retention exceptions. See the Privacy Policy.
