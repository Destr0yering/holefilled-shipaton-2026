# UKG Sector Demo Strategy

HoleFilled demos use fictional employers and workers. Public UKG customer stories inform the operational patterns only; they do not imply that a named organization uses, endorses, or integrates with HoleFilled.

## Demonstrated sectors

| HoleFilled demo | Operational emphasis | Public UKG evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registered nurse | License and unit-competency filtering, central staffing, safe decline, differential and transportation policy | [Bon Secours Mercy Health](https://www.ukg.com/customers/bon-secours-mercy-health), [Hartford HealthCare](https://www.ukg.com/customers/hartford-healthcare), and [Billings Clinic](https://www.ukg.com/customer-stories/billings-clinic) describe large-scale scheduling, float staffing, self-scheduling, and coverage visibility. |
| Forklift operator | Certification, night-shift availability, frontline absenteeism, commute support | UKG's [manufacturing overview](https://www.ukg.com/learn/article-library/executive-leaders/why-ukg-workforce-solution-built-manufacturing) highlights frontline absenteeism and open-shift workflows; its customer directory includes distribution and manufacturing organizations. |
| Banquet server | Event start deadline, alcohol-service training, same-day premium and downtown transportation | UKG's [hospitality and food-service overview](https://www.ukg.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/Why%20UKG%20for%20Hospitality%20and%20Food%20Service.pdf) emphasizes scheduling flexibility and reducing manager scheduling work. |
| Line cook | Food-handler qualification, dinner-rush urgency, parking/fuel barrier | The same hospitality and food-service evidence supports high-volume frontline scheduling and manager productivity use cases. |
| Crossing guard | Board-authorized roster, post conflict detection, student-safety deadline, district transport | [San Juan School District](https://www.ukg.com/customers/san-juan-school-district) and [Columbus City Schools](https://www.ukg.com/customers/columbus-city-schools) document UKG workforce management across school employees, substitutes, hourly staff, bus drivers, absence reporting, and complex rules. |

## Demo positioning

- Say “UKG-ready canonical event and adapter boundary,” not “certified UKG integration.”
- Use fictional names, identifiers, worksites, employees, and results in every live demo.
- Describe eligibility as employer-defined and deterministic. AI may converse and classify barriers but cannot invent credentials.
- For healthcare, revalidate active licensure, competencies, rest rules, conflicts, and staffing policy before any assignment.
- For public-sector education, the school board remains responsible for background checks, authorization, policy, and worker records. This is workforce coverage, not the separate future Got2Get2School product.
- Never use protected characteristics for ranking or persuasion, and never imply a penalty for declining.
