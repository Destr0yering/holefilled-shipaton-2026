# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

HoleFilled

## Short description

Resolve urgent staffing gaps with controlled outreach and transportation support.

## Full description

When a worker calls out, HoleFilled helps an authorized manager recover the open shift.

HoleFilled identifies qualified coworkers from the employer's internal workforce, coordinates policy-controlled outreach, captures barriers that prevent acceptance, and returns a confirmed assignment. When transportation is the obstacle, an employer can offer approved ride assistance or coordinate through a configured mobility provider.

Key capabilities:

- Review urgent open shifts and qualified internal coworkers.
- Coordinate bounded SMS outreach and employer-optional voice workflows.
- Understand common barriers such as transportation or shift-premium needs.
- Apply employer-defined incentive limits and manager approvals.
- Confirm one eligible acceptance and close competing outreach.
- Preserve an accountable decision timeline.
- Unlock Pro automation through a RevenueCat-managed subscription.

AI assists with conversation and barrier classification. Employer policies and deterministic rules control qualification, spending, and assignment. Workers can decline without penalty, and production deployments must honor communication consent and opt-out preferences.

The initial public release should describe only integrations that are actually enabled. Do not claim certified UKG, Uber, Lyft, or other partnerships without written authorization.
