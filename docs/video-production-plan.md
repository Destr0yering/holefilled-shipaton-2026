# HoleFilled Shipaton video production plan

**Format:** 35 seconds, 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps.  
**Style:** Fast, cinematic anime cutaways around authentic app footage. The generated animation represents the scheduling problem and outcome; the real app recording proves the product.

## Core message

An uncovered role is not just an empty shift. HoleFilled lets a manager quickly spot the gap, fill it, and keep the team on track.

## Editing notes

- Use the app capture as evidence, not as a background: keep each product shot on screen 4–7 seconds and avoid speed-ramping it.
- Use a white flash or phone-screen match cut between anime and the real app; no more than two-frame flash.
- Keep the app UI untouched. Add any red/green emphasis as an editable overlay, not inside the capture.
- Use a royalty-free upbeat electronic beat around 100–115 BPM. Start sparse; let the beat drop at the fill confirmation.
- Burn in captions matching the spoken copy for accessibility.
- Export an H.264 MP4, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 12–20 Mbps, with AAC audio at 48 kHz.

## Home capture checklist

Capture in landscape with the device/browser notification-free. Do three clean takes of each moment; pause one second before and after each action.

1. **Gap screen (6–8 seconds):** Open the schedule with one uncovered role clearly visible.
2. **Fill interaction (8–12 seconds):** From the same schedule, perform the exact manager action that fills or assigns the role. Do not cut during the action.
3. **Confirmed schedule (6–8 seconds):** Show the now-covered role and any confirmation state.
4. **Optional hero screen (5 seconds):** Home/dashboard or the most visually polished app screen for the end card.

Avoid showing real employee names, phone numbers, emails, or sensitive schedule data. Use demo data if possible.
